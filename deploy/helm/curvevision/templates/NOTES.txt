CurveVision {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed as {{ include "curvevision.fullname" . }}.

{{- if .Values.ingress.enabled }}
Open https://{{ .Values.ingress.host }}/ and register the first account.
{{- else }}
Reach it with:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "curvevision.fullname" . }}-web 8080:80
and open http://localhost:8080/ to register the first account.
{{- end }}

Back up the database and storage together, with one command:
  kubectl exec deploy/{{ include "curvevision.fullname" . }}-api -- curvevision-server backup /tmp/cv.tar
(then copy it out with kubectl cp). docs/OPERATIONS.md has the rest.
